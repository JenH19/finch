Worksheet

The 15-Minute
Clarity Reset

A printable practice for when something feels off, without pressure, wasted time, or the need to fix anything immediately.

Print the worksheet → Back to the guide →
How to use it

Set a timer for 15 minutes and let your answers unfold.

1. What feels off?

Use a few sentences or a list to capture the feeling you can’t quite name.

2. What is true for me right now?

Notice one honest thing about your current life, energy, or priorities.

3. What needs more space?

What would feel better if it had more room in your week, your mindset, or your heart?

4. What do I want more of?

Choose one feeling, experience, or quality you want to invite back in.

5. One small next step

Pick one thing you can do in the next 24 hours that feels nourishing and possible.

Use this worksheet again whenever you want to check in with yourself and move from stuck to subtle momentum.
