Thursday, October 22, 2026 | 6:00–8:00 PM | Point Loma

The Perch Gathering

You don’t need a five-year plan. You might just need to figure out your next move.

Join us for the October edition of The Perch, a small, welcoming gathering for women who want space to step out of the day-to-day, connect with other women, and think a little more intentionally about what’s next.

This month, we’re exploring The Next Right Thing: the decisions, conversations, experiments, and small shifts that can move us toward something that feels more aligned, interesting, or simply more us.

Expect guided reflection, meaningful conversation, plenty of connection, and absolutely no pressure to have your life figured out.

Come as you are. Leave with a little more clarity about where you might go from here.

Your first drink and light bites are included.

Space is intentionally limited to keep the conversation personal.

Sunday, October 11 | Meet at 2:45 PM, depart at 3:00 PM | Mission Bay

The Perch: Golden Hour Cruise

A special edition of The Perch, this time on the water.

Join us for a late-afternoon cruise on Mission Bay as we make our way toward golden hour. This one will be intentionally unstructured: no facilitated discussion, no worksheets, and no pressure to arrive with anything figured out. Just a beautiful afternoon, some drinks and snacks, and time to relax and connect with other Perchers.

We’ll meet at 2:45 PM and depart promptly at 3:00 PM. Final return time and marina details will be shared with registered guests.

Space is limited to 10 women. Registration is $20 and includes the boat, fuel, drinks, and light snacks. Because boating plans depend on weather and conditions on the water, we’ll confirm final details closer to the date.
