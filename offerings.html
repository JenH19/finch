Ways to work together

Clarity doesn’t always come from trying harder or making a plan right away. Often it arrives when you have a different kind of space: the right questions, a thoughtful conversation, and people who make room for honest answers, and, occasionally, a well-timed laugh.

1:1 Coaching

Four sessions. Two months. Space to figure out what’s next.

This is the primary way most clients begin working with Bright Finch. If something feels ready to shift, a career nudge, how you spend your time, or simple curiosity about the next chapter, we’ll get under the noise, ask better questions, and leave the “shoulds” at the door.

Across four 60-minute sessions, we’ll clarify what matters, explore possibilities, and turn small insights into intentional movement, without pressure to have it all figured out on day one.

  • Four 60-minute private coaching sessions
  • Two-month coaching partnership
  • Reflection prompts and practical tools when useful
  • Support between sessions
  • Complimentary seat at an upcoming Perch gathering

The Perch

A modern third space for thoughtful women

The Perch is Bright Finch’s in-person community experience, not a networking event or a workshop. It’s a relaxed, intentional gathering that mixes quiet reflection, guided conversation, and the kind of connection that actually matters.

Come for an evening of real talk, gentle prompts, and company that helps you hear yourself think. Bring curiosity; heels optional.

An immersive week in Southwest France
