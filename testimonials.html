What Women Are Saying
About Jen's Coaching

Women come to Bright Finch with a lot on their plates and a lot on their minds. What they often leave with is a little more clarity, a little more ease, and a lot more trust in themselves.

“My session with Jen gave me a physical sense of release. I was finally able to say what I wanted for myself and realize that I could allow myself to end a role without guilt. It was freeing.”

“I feel empowered to take my life back. The coaching and follow-on group discussions were beyond helpful in deciding what I need to focus on and what needs to change.”

“I’ve done plenty of travel, reflection and coaching before, but this was on a whole different level. I was guided through deep personal and professional introspection and came away with important insights.”

“I came away feeling embraced, heard and uplifted. I uncovered something about my own self-worth that I hadn’t recognized before. I feel strengthened by that discovery and hopeful about the steps I can take for my own well-being.”

“For the first time, perhaps ever, I was thinking with my heart as well as my head. I saw options I’d never before considered for myself.”

“Even though I came in knowing no one, I felt so safe to share myself and my thoughts. I think that has a lot to do with who Jen is and the way she supports women.”

“Through our conversations, I came to the conclusion that I don’t need any additional change right now. That was impactful.”

“The thoughtful questions asked us to think, but not necessarily solve anything for each other. We simply held space for one another.”

“The coaching gave me time and permission to work on myself and the space to examine my priorities to make sure they’re in alignment as I move forward.”

“The gently guided coaching gave us space to turn inward individually and then share our thoughts, doubts and hopes. From the beginning, I knew I was in a safe space.”
